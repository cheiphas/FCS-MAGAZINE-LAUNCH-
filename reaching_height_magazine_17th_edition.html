About the Publication

A Publication with Purpose

Reaching Height is the premier annual spiritual journal published by the Fellowship of Christian Students (FCS), Bayero University Kano.

Our Vision & Purpose

For seventeen consecutive editions, Reaching Height has served as a spiritual lighthouse on campus, equipping students and believers with profound biblical insights, doctrinal clarity, and testimonies of God's grace.

This edition addresses the pressing realities of Christian faith in academic spaces, strengthening resolve and fostering steadfast devotion.

“Designed to inspire reflection, deepen fellowship, and elevate our spiritual walk.”

What Readers Can Expect

  • Deep scriptural expositions centered on the 17th edition theme.
  • Inspirational essays and articles addressing student life and integrity.
  • Edifying poetry, devotionals, and campus testimonies.
  • Commemorative layout reflecting 17 years of unwavering campus ministry.
17th Edition Theme Spotlight

“HIS PRESENCE, OUR CONFIDENCE”

In a world marked by uncertainty and rapid changes, the abiding presence of Almighty God remains the ultimate anchor for the believer. This theme explores how divine fellowship breeds holy boldness, unwavering faith, and enduring peace in our daily pursuits.
